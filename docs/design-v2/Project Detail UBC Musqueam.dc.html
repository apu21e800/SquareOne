UBC × Musqueam Crosswalk

Vancouver · DecoMark · 2023
Municipality
Vancouver · UBC campus
Year
2023
Service
Preformed thermoplastic
Products used
DecoMark custom graphics

The brief asked for a pedestrian crossing that carries a Musqueam artwork at road scale, at a campus intersection on a bus route that could not stay closed for long. The line work had to survive translation from the artists’ files to a surface driven over thousands of times a day.

We reproduced the design in DecoMark preformed thermoplastic, cut off-site to the supplied artwork and laid out panel by panel on the closed intersection. The full crossing was heat-fused into the asphalt over two night closures, and buses were running over it by each morning.

Three years of buses, bikes and Vancouver rain later, the crossing reads as installed. One panel was replaced after a utility cut in 2025; the patch is not visible from the curb.
