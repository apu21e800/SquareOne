Practical notes

Surface prep

New asphalt needs about 30 days to cure before thermoplastic goes down. Existing surfaces are cleaned first, and vapour blasted where old paint or oils would interfere with the bond.

Cure windows

Preformed thermoplastic opens to traffic as it cools, usually within the hour. Coated colour fields need 24 to 48 dry hours, so we sequence them ahead of the markings.

Traffic management

We carry our own traffic control people and signage for single-lane and full closures, and stage school-zone work inside summer and holiday breaks.
