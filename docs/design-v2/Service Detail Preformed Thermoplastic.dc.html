Process

Five steps, one crew

01

Site walk

We measure, photograph and flag substrate issues on site.

02

Specification

Pattern, colours and product matched to traffic and budget.

03

Surface prep

Cleaning, and vapour blasting where the surface needs it.

04

Application

Sheets heat-fused into the pavement by our own crews.

05

Cure & walk-through

Open to traffic as it cools; we walk the finished work with you.
