Questions we hear

Most driveways run about a week end to end: prep, imprint, two coats, cure. You can usually park on it 48 hours after the final coat.

The coating flexes with the asphalt through freeze-thaw instead of cracking the way rigid surfaces can. It shovels and plows like ordinary asphalt.

Sweep it, rinse it, and plan a recoat every 8 to 10 years to refresh the colour and protection. No sanding, sealing or weeding between.

Typically around 60 percent of the installed cost of concrete pavers, and there are no joints to settle, shift or grow weeds.
