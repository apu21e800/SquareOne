StreetBond

A water-based, epoxy-modified acrylic coating that colours and protects asphalt and concrete, in standard and custom colours.

Request a quote
Key benefits

Holds its colour

UV-stable pigments stand up to sun, rain and winter maintenance.

Grips underfoot

Textured finish keeps traction through coastal wet seasons.

Moves with the pavement

Flexes with asphalt rather than cracking away from it.

Renews in place

Recoats over the existing surface, with no removal or repaving.

Spec facts
System
Epoxy-modified acrylic, water-based
Substrates
Asphalt, concrete, stamped asphalt
Colours
30 standard, custom on request
Finish
Matte, with anti-skid texture
Return to traffic
24–48 hours, temperature dependent
Maintenance
Periodic recoat, no removal
