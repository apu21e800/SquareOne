# A letter before you build — creative north star
Source: HUBSS project agents, 29 Aug 2026, relayed by Vern. Binding as
creative direction alongside S1-BUILD-PROMPT.md (rules) and
docs/SEO-BRIEF.md (strategy). Where they conflict, the rules win; where
they don't, this letter is the compass.

THE COMPASS: HUB sells confidence-to-specify. Square One sells
TRUST-IN-A-CREW. The buyer is spending $15-30k once, with a crew they've
never met, on a surface they'll park on for twenty years. Every design
decision either builds that trust or spends it.

THE FUNNEL, four beats in the visitor's words:
1. "Oh — that's possible?"        -> gallery as category education,
                                     lead with transformation
2. "Could that be MY driveway?"   -> before/after is the entire genre;
                                     same house, same angle, new surface
3. "Can I trust these people?"    -> the crew, the 25 years, the process
                                     like a neighbour would tell it;
                                     real photos of real people, no stock
4. "What's the easy first step?"  -> a zero-cost offer that feels like a
                                     gift (PROPOSE to owners, never ship
                                     unblessed — see docs/PROPOSALS-owners.md)
A healthy funnel has no dead ends: every page knows which beat it serves
and hands the visitor the next one. A page that can't say what it's FOR
is furniture.

THE FRANCHISE IDEA (proposed, needs owners): "STILL STANDING." Revisit
installs from 2005 / 2012 / 2018, photograph them today beside the install
shots. Unfakeable proof, uncopyable content, and pride for the owners.

OWNER-SENSITIVE: bold in design, conservative in claims. Radical layouts,
careful sentences. When ambition and owner comfort collide, show both
versions and let the humans decide. Earn slack, then use it.

ON EDGE: edgy means point of view, not volume. This trade takes the most
ignored surface in the built world and makes it worth looking at. If the
design would work for a dentist, it has no edge. If it could only be about
surfaces built by hand in BC weather, that's it.

FUNNEL AUDIT vs current build (Cowork, 29 Aug):
- Beat 1: STRONG. Rockstar hero + galleries lead with transformation.
- Beat 2: MISSING. No before/after genre anywhere on the site. Needs
  paired photography (owners supply) + a before/after component.
- Beat 3: PARTIAL. Process + 25 years present; NO crew photography, no
  faces. Needs a real shoot (see PROPOSALS shot list).
- Beat 4: DEFAULT ONLY. "Request a quote" / "Book a free site visit"
  exist; the gift-offer is unproposed. Three candidates await owner
  blessing in docs/PROPOSALS-owners.md.
