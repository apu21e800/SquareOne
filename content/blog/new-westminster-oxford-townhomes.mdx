The Oxford Townhomes development in New Westminster received decorative asphalt surface work — driveway and pathway coatings for a strata residential community that prioritized a finished, premium appearance at the common areas and individual unit driveways.

New Westminster is a dense urban municipality where residential developments exist in close proximity, and the exterior finish of a development is visible to pedestrians and neighbouring properties. Getting the surface right at Oxford Townhomes contributed to the project's visual presentation in its urban context.

## Strata Development Surface Specifications

Strata developments have a particular dynamic when it comes to surface specifications: the common area surfaces are maintained collectively, which means the initial specification choice has long-term cost implications for the strata corporation.

Decorative asphalt coatings provide a strong argument for strata applications:

- **Lower long-term maintenance cost** compared to unit pavers (no settling, no weed growth between units, no individual unit replacement)
- **Consistent appearance** across all common areas — no variation in material weathering that creates visible patchwork over time
- **Faster installation** during the original construction phase, reducing project timeline
- **Recoat flexibility** — when the coating eventually reaches the end of its service life, a recoat job is significantly less expensive and disruptive than pavement replacement

For the Oxford Townhomes strata corporation, the specification represents a combination of initial visual quality and long-term cost management.
