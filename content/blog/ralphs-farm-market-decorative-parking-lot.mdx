Ralph's Farm Market on Fraser Highway in Murrayville is a BC institution — what started as a seasonal hay wagon in 1982 grew into a year-round 11,000 square foot market. When they upgraded the parking lot, they wanted the surface to reflect the care they put into the rest of the operation.

Square One Paving and HUB Surface Systems worked together on this one: a custom stamped asphalt design that ties in store branding and creates an outdoor retail experience that matches the market's character.

## Stamped Asphalt for Commercial Parking

Commercial parking lots are a significant surface area in most retail operations — often the first thing a customer walks across and the context that shapes their first impression of the business. A well-designed parking surface communicates that the operator cares about the details.

For Ralph's Farm Market, the brief was to create a one-of-a-kind surface that feels intentional — an impressive landscape that reinforces the market's brand while functioning as a practical parking surface for customers and delivery vehicles.

We used StreetPrint stamping to create the pattern in the asphalt substrate, followed by StreetBond coatings in colours selected to complement the market's palette. The result is a parking lot that functions correctly — good drainage, clear lane markings, accessible pathways — while looking like something a designer specified.

## The Branding Integration

Incorporating store branding into a parking lot surface is a detail that not every operator considers, but the payoff is real: the surface becomes part of the overall brand experience rather than just functional infrastructure. Customers who visit Ralph's regularly notice the consistency between the interior presentation and the exterior surfaces.
