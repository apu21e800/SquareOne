The second phase of driveway work at Windsor Gate, Polygon Realty's masterplanned community in Coquitlam — returning to a project we know well, delivering the same stamped asphalt and StreetBond coating system to the next phase of the development.

Multi-phase projects like Windsor Gate reflect the kind of relationship we build with developers who have found a specification that works. Phase one delivered the visual standard and performance that Polygon required. Phase two expanded that across additional lots, maintaining perfect consistency with the existing installation.

## Consistency Across Development Phases

The primary technical challenge in returning to a phased project after a gap is colour matching. StreetBond formulations need to match phase one exactly — if the colours drift even slightly between phases, it's visible when the two phases are seen together in the completed development.

We maintain complete batch records and colour specifications from every phase of a project specifically to enable accurate reproduction. For Windsor Gate phase two, the match was exact.

## The Polygon Realty Standard

Polygon is one of BC's most recognized residential developers — a company whose product quality is visible in the details. Specifying stamped asphalt driveways rather than plain concrete or asphalt is one of those details: it signals that the developer has considered every surface the buyer will interact with.

StreetPrint stamping creates an impression pattern (cobblestone, herringbone, or similar) in the asphalt while it's still hot. StreetBond coatings applied after curing add the colour layer that gives the driveway its distinctive finish. The result is a surface that reads premium without the cost, weight, or installation complexity of natural stone or unit pavers.
