## StreetPrint + StreetBond for Residential Driveways

The StreetPrint and StreetBond combination produces a residential driveway surface that rivals the appearance of natural stone or unit pavers while outperforming both in BC's climate conditions.

**StreetPrint** is applied to hot asphalt, impressing a defined texture pattern — cobblestone, herringbone, European fan, or other profiles — while the material is still workable. The pattern creates depth and visual interest that bare asphalt cannot replicate.

**StreetBond** is applied after the asphalt has cured, providing the colour coat and protective sealant layer that defines the finished appearance. The range of available colours allows precise matching to the development's architectural palette.

The combined service life for a residential driveway application — assuming standard maintenance — is typically fifteen to twenty years before a recoat is warranted. The asphalt substrate beneath the coating may last considerably longer.
