Parc Riviera Mews is a townhouse development by DAVA Developments at 10199 River Drive in Richmond — a project that specified StreetBond decorative asphalt coatings for the walkway and hardscape surfaces during construction.

StreetBond is increasingly the product of choice for developer-specified decorative surfaces in townhouse and strata projects. The combination of cost efficiency relative to stone or concrete alternatives, strong visual quality, and long service life makes the value case straightforward for developers who want to deliver a premium finish without premium materials budgets.

## What StreetBond Delivers at the Developer Scale

For a development like Parc Riviera Mews, the surface specification affects every resident's daily experience — the walkway from the parking area, the driveway apron, the common area pathways. These are the surfaces people see and walk on every day.

StreetBond coatings create what we describe as an outstanding surface application: attractive, durable, and highly visible. The colour options allow the developer to specify a palette that complements the architecture and landscaping. The durability — typically eight to twelve years to first recoat under residential use — means the development maintains its finished appearance through multiple resident cycles without the developer carrying ongoing maintenance costs.

## Application Process

The work at Parc Riviera Mews was phased with the broader construction timeline. StreetBond is applied after the asphalt substrate has cured — we coordinate with the general contractor to ensure the surface is ready before we mobilize. Prep (cleaning, priming where required), coat, aggregate embed, and seal. The surface is typically ready for foot traffic within twenty-four hours and vehicle traffic within forty-eight.
