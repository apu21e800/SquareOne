The fire lane at the Maplewoods development in North Vancouver received a StreetBond coating in Safety Blue — a specification that fulfils the functional fire access marking requirement while delivering a surface finish that reads as an intentional architectural element rather than industrial safety infrastructure.

Fire lanes in residential strata and multi-family developments present a recurring design challenge: the lane has to be clearly identifiable as a fire access route (BC fire code requirements are specific about marking), but the colouring required can visually disrupt an otherwise well-designed exterior environment.

## Blue as a Design Solution

Safety Blue is increasingly the preferred fire lane colour specification for architects and developers who are thinking carefully about the exterior character of their buildings. Red fire lanes are the traditional specification, but blue reads as a more contemporary, architectural colour choice — one that works with the grey-and-glass aesthetic of modern strata buildings rather than fighting against it.

The Maplewoods installation demonstrates what this looks like in practice: a fire lane that is clearly identifiable, code-compliant, and visually integrated into the building's exterior design language.

## StreetBond for Strata Fire Lanes

StreetBond is the appropriate product for a fire lane application because:

- The colour is consistent across the full surface — no patchwork from multiple application passes
- The finish is durable enough to withstand the occasional heavy vehicle use that fire lanes see (delivery vehicles, emergency apparatus)
- The aggregate surface provides traction in the wet North Shore conditions that this location experiences for most of the year
- The service life is measured in years, not seasons — the maintenance burden on the strata corporation is minimal
