Working with the Kitsilano Community Centre Association, the Kitsilano War Memorial Community Centre, members of the community, and Space2place landscape architecture, the Vancouver Park Board renewed the spray park surface at Connaught Park using StreetBond coatings.

Connaught Park is a well-used Kitsilano neighbourhood park. The spray park is a summer anchor for families in the area — a location that sees concentrated use from June through September, with significant foot traffic from children running across the surface repeatedly throughout the day.

## Spray Park Surface Requirements

Spray parks have specific surface requirements that make StreetBond a well-suited specification. The combination of water saturation, children running barefoot, UV exposure, and the wear from constant foot traffic demands a coating system that:

- Remains slip-resistant when wet (aggregate finish is non-negotiable)
- Holds colour under intense summer UV without bleaching or chalking
- Is completely non-toxic and safe for skin contact
- Can be detailed in multiple colours to support playful design layouts

StreetBond meets all of these. The formulation is used in water park and spray pad applications across North America, including multiple installations we've completed in Burnaby and other Lower Mainland municipalities.

## Community-Led Design

The Connaught Park renewal was notable for the community involvement in the design process. The Kitsilano Community Centre Association, local residents, and the landscape architecture team at Space2place collaborated on the visual design before we were engaged to install it.

Working from a design that had community buy-in meant the installation brief was clear and the final result reflected what the neighbourhood actually wanted for the space.
