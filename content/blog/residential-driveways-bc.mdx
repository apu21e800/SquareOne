Your driveway is the first thing people see when they pull up to your home. Before they notice the fresh trim, the landscaping, or the front door — they've already formed an impression. In BC, where homes are a serious investment and curb appeal has real dollar value at resale, that impression matters more than most homeowners realize.

At Square One Paving, we've been doing residential driveways across the Lower Mainland and Vancouver Island for over 25 years. We've seen every condition this province can throw at a surface — and we've learned exactly what separates an install that holds up from one that doesn't.

Here's what a premium residential driveway actually looks like, and what you should expect from anyone doing this kind of work.

## BC's Climate Is Not Forgiving

The freeze-thaw cycles in the Fraser Valley. The persistent wet seasons on Vancouver Island. The weeks of standing water that pool on flat Lower Mainland lots before they have a chance to drain. This province is hard on surfaces that weren't designed for it.

We've seen driveways fail within a few years because a contractor chose the wrong product, applied it at the wrong temperature, or skipped the base assessment entirely. They look fine on day one. The cracking starts at the edges by year two. By year three, the colour has gone patchy in the UV and the repair cost is starting to look like a full redo.

A proper BC install starts before any product goes down: drainage assessment, base condition review, and selecting a system that was actually engineered for our climate. Not a system designed for California or Ontario — one that flexes with our freeze-thaw cycles and sheds BC rain the way it should.

## What We Install

Depending on the condition of your existing surface, your budget, and the look you're after, there are a few directions we typically go.

**StreetBond Decorative Coating**

If your existing asphalt is structurally sound, StreetBond is a premium colour coating that transforms the surface without a full replacement. Dozens of tones — warm terracotta, deep charcoal, slate grey — applied in a single day, curing in 24 hours. It adds real grip underfoot, protects the asphalt underneath, and lasts 5–8 years before a simple recoat. This is the smart upgrade when you want a clean, finished look at a reasonable cost.

**StreetPrint Stamped Asphalt**

This is the product that stops people on the street. StreetPrint patterns — brick, cobblestone, herringbone, slate — are stamped directly into fresh asphalt. The result looks like premium hardscape. Structurally, it's asphalt: flexible, freeze-thaw resistant, and snowplow-safe. Visually, it's something else entirely. Gated estate driveways, heritage homes with circular entries, townhome complexes looking to elevate their streetscape — this is where StreetPrint earns its reputation. We'd call it our flagship residential product. Service life of 20+ years with proper maintenance.

**Vapor Blasted Surface Prep**

Not every driveway needs a new surface — sometimes it needs to be prepared properly for one. Square One is BC's only mobile vapor blasting service. We use water-assisted abrasive blasting to strip old coatings, remove oil staining, and clean surfaces to bare asphalt before a new system goes down. No silica dust, no harsh chemicals. A cleaner result than pressure washing alone, and a better base for whatever comes next.

## What a Premium Install Actually Means

The difference between a quality job and a budget one is rarely obvious on day one. It shows up in year three when the edges are peeling, the joints are cracking, and the surface has aged in ways that cost real money to fix.

Premium means:

- Base assessment before any product is selected
- Proper edge preparation and drainage transitions
- Climate-appropriate sealers applied at the correct temperature window
- Consistent coverage — no thin spots, no lap marks, no corners cut on material quantity
- A crew experienced enough to spot problems before they become your problem

We install certified Canadian-engineered decorative pavement systems — StreetPrint, StreetBond, DuraShield, and TrafficPatterns — designed for BC climate conditions and backed by real technical support. We've been installing these systems since the beginning, which means we install to specification, not approximation.
