The fourth and final phase of construction at Rutland Centennial Park in Kelowna brought us back for the completion of a multi-phase decorative asphalt project. StreetBond coatings were the specification throughout: cost-effective, visually strong, and suited to the Okanagan's climate demands.

Rutland Centennial Park is a community park in the Rutland neighbourhood of Kelowna — a well-used space that serves residents across a wide age range. The phased improvement program allowed the City to upgrade the surface in stages, managing budget and minimizing disruption to park users.

## Multi-Phase Project Delivery

Phased projects have specific requirements that single-site installs don't. Colour consistency between phases is critical — the StreetBond formulations for phase four had to match what was applied in phases one through three, so the finished park reads as a unified design rather than a patchwork of different eras.

We maintain records of the exact specifications — product batches, colour codes, aggregate specifications — from each phase of a multi-year project precisely to enable this consistency. When phase four was approved, we matched to the existing installation.

## Okanagan Climate Performance

Kelowna's climate presents different challenges from the Lower Mainland. Summer UV is intense — the Okanagan sees considerably more sunshine hours than Metro Vancouver — and the combination of hot summers and cold winters creates a more demanding freeze-thaw cycle for surface coatings.

StreetBond's formulation performs well in this environment. The UV-stable colour system maintains saturation through Okanagan summer conditions. We've tracked comparable installations in Kelowna and the broader Thompson-Okanagan that hold up consistently over five-plus year periods.
