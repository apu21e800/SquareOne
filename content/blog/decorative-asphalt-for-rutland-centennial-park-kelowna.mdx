The fourth and final phase of construction at Rutland Centennial Park in Kelowna brought us back for the completion of a multi-phase decorative asphalt project. StreetBond® coatings were the specification throughout — cost-effective, visually strong, and suited to the Okanagan's climate demands — with DecoMark medallions set into the coloured fields.

Rutland Centennial Park is a community park in the Rutland neighbourhood of Kelowna — a well-used space that serves residents across a wide age range. The phased improvement program allowed the City to upgrade the surface in stages, managing budget and minimizing disruption to park users.

The lede photograph is an aerial of the finished plaza. ## Multi-phase project delivery

Phased projects have specific requirements that single-site installs don't. Colour consistency between phases is critical — the StreetBond formulations for phase four had to match what was applied in phases one through three, so the finished park reads as a unified design rather than a patchwork of different eras.

We maintain records of the exact specifications — product batches, colour codes, aggregate specifications — from each phase of a multi-year project precisely to enable this consistency. When phase four was approved, we matched to the existing installation.

## Okanagan climate performance

Kelowna's climate presents different challenges from the Lower Mainland. Summer UV is intense — the Okanagan sees considerably more sunshine hours than Metro Vancouver — and the combination of hot summers and cold winters creates a more demanding freeze-thaw cycle for surface coatings.

StreetBond's formulation is built for this environment: the manufacturer describes the coating as UV-stable, and the anti-skid aggregate holds grip through the wet months. Our other Interior work in the same material includes a [solar-reflective StreetBond SR pathway in Osoyoos](/projects/osoyoos-park-pathway), installed in 2023.

## For a parks department phasing a project

- **Record the colour codes at phase one.** Matching a coating years later is straightforward when the specification is on file and guesswork when it isn't.
- **Let the curbs do the zoning.** Where a plaza has raised edges, run each colour to the curb; the joints stay clean and a future recoat can be done zone by zone.
- **Put the medallions where feet go.** DecoMark holds its edge under foot traffic, so the graphics can sit in the path rather than off to the side.
