Grandview Heights School specified StreetPrint® stamped asphalt for a new crosswalk installation — creating a high-visibility, defined crossing that improves road safety in the school zone.

School crosswalks are an application where the functional and aesthetic requirements align completely. A crosswalk that is visually distinctive from its surroundings causes drivers to slow earlier. A crosswalk that maintains that distinctiveness year-round — rather than fading to near-invisible after one winter — provides consistent safety benefit.

## What the photograph shows

The lede photograph is the crossing on a clear morning, with the new school grounds behind it. The field of the crosswalk is a red brick pattern, and a broad yellow border runs along each side of it from curb to curb, so the crossing reads as a red block bracketed in yellow against the grey of the road. A pedestrian sign stands at the near curb; a covered structure and young trees are visible beyond. There is no white paint anywhere in the crossing — the colour and the texture are in the asphalt itself. The [project record](/projects/grandview-heights-school-crosswalk) holds a second frame.

## Why StreetPrint for school crosswalks

StreetPrint is the stamped asphalt system we specify for school crosswalk applications for several reasons.

The stamping process creates a textured pattern in the asphalt that reads differently from the surrounding road surface even in the absence of colour — there's a tonal and textural contrast that registers before a driver even processes the colour cues. When StreetBond® colour coatings are added on top of the StreetPrint substrate, the visual impact increases substantially.

The combination is visible from the approach distances that matter for school zone safety — the stretch of road where a driver is making a speed decision. Standard white paint crosswalk markings don't achieve this level of early visibility, particularly in wet conditions.

## The system

Square One installs StreetPrint under our [stamped asphalt](/services/stamped-asphalt) service. The manufacturer's published facts: a heated template is pressed into new or existing asphalt, so the pattern is built into the surface rather than painted onto it, with nothing to peel and nothing to re-lay; the texture is slip-resistant and snowplow and de-icing salt safe; custom patterns and colours are available, with retroreflective options for crosswalks; and the manufacturer publishes a 10–20 year service life. StreetBond supplies the colour, in more than 50 standard colours plus custom mixing, with an anti-skid aggregate and an 8+ year life cycle that is refreshed rather than replaced. Specifications: [StreetPrint](/products/streetprint) and [StreetBond](/products/streetbond); the [crosswalks gallery](/applications/crosswalks) shows the range from high-visibility brick like this one to rainbow and artist-designed crossings.

## School zone safety context

BC's school zones have seen steady safety investment in recent years, and decorative and high-visibility crosswalk systems are part of that investment — engineering the environment to produce safer driver behaviour, rather than relying solely on enforcement.

Grandview Heights is one of a growing number of BC schools that have specified decorative crosswalk systems. The case for them is practical: a crossing drivers notice earlier, and a surface that performs for years without the maintenance burden of painted alternatives. Where a school needs the full package — crossings plus legends, symbols and pathway lines — our [South Langford Elementary](/blog/school-zone-thermoplastic-bc) work shows what preformed thermoplastic adds.

## For a school district or municipality

- **Choose the colours for contrast, not decoration.** Red with yellow borders, as here, is a signal; the [schools and sports courts gallery](/applications/schools-sports-courts) shows the same logic on other sites.
- **Ask about retroreflective options.** The manufacturer offers them for StreetPrint crosswalks, and a school zone that operates in November darkness may want them.
- **Book the summer.** A crossing imprinted and coated while the school is empty is open before the first bell.
