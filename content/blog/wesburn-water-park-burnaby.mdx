Wesburn Park in Burnaby received a new spray pad surface in October 2023 — StreetBond® durable pavement coatings installed to create a bold, colourful, long-lasting water play surface for the neighbourhood park.

Wesburn Park is a community park in South Burnaby. The spray pad addition is a significant amenity upgrade for the families who use the park through the summer months. We were engaged to deliver the surface installation — the coating that makes a spray pad functional, safe, and visually engaging for children.

## What the surface looks like

Square One's photographs of Wesburn Park, filed in the [parks and paths gallery](/applications/parks-paths), show the finished pad in summer. The main field is a pale, near-white coating, and a teal stream winds across it from one side of the pad to the other, passing under three spray arches and through a purple circle where two boulders flank a small water feature. Purple leaf shapes are scattered across the white as if they had fallen there. The colour is the play narrative — a creek to follow, stones to stand on, a pool at the end — carried flush with the surface.

## Why StreetBond leads spray pad applications

StreetBond has become our go-to specification for spray pad and water play surfaces in BC, and for good reason. The product consistently outperforms alternatives in the specific conditions that spray pads generate:

**Permanent water saturation** — A spray pad surface is wet for its entire operating season. Coatings that don't tolerate constant moisture fail early. StreetBond's chemistry is designed for wet outdoor applications.

**Intense UV** — Summer spray pad hours coincide with peak UV exposure. Colour systems that aren't UV-stable bleach noticeably within a single season. StreetBond's colour formulation maintains saturation through full BC summer conditions.

**Barefoot safety** — Aggregate embed in the coating provides traction without roughness — the balance between slip resistance and comfort for running children is something we calibrate specifically for spray pad applications.

## Wesburn Park, October 2023

The October installation timeline allowed the surface to fully cure before the park closed for winter, ensuring it would be ready for full-season use the following summer. Planning the installation in the shoulder season is our standard approach for spray pad projects — it minimizes disruption to park users and maximizes cure time before the surface sees its first summer load.

Wesburn joins the Burnaby spray pads in our record: the StreetBond 150 recoat at [Keswick Water Park](/blog/keswick-waterpark-burnaby) and the [Confederation Park](/blog/confederation-water-park-burnaby) water feature designed with Space2place. Across the region there is [Connaught Park](/blog/connaught-park-spray-park-renewal) in Kitsilano and a [Maple Ridge spray park](/projects/maple-ridge-spray-park) installed in July 2025.

## For a parks department planning a spray pad

- **Design the colour as the play.** A stream, a pool and stepping stones give children a story to run; the coating can carry any layout the landscape architect draws.
- **Plan the recoat from day one.** Colour is the first thing to go on a pad that is wet all summer; a recoat over the existing surface, as at Keswick, restores it without a rebuild.
- **Book the shoulder season.** Coat in the fall or spring, cure through the closure, open on time.
